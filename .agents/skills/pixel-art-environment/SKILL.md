# Pixel-art environment

Use this skill when creating, integrating, organizing, or reviewing scene, sprite, animation, or other visual assets for the portfolio. Read `docs/planejamento.md` and inspect the approved references in `docs/referencias/` before changing visual work.

## Approved visual constraints

- Treat `docs/referencias/personagem-aprovado.png` and `docs/referencias/cenario-aprovado.png` as the official concept references.
- Do not redesign, modify, or replace the approved character or scene concepts without explicit user approval. Preserve the character's approved appearance and the landscape's clean, continuous horizontal composition.
- Keep the six approved landmarks and their meanings: entry portal/presentation, sign/trajectory, computer/skills, workbench/contributions, bookshelf/education, and crystal/contact. Small spacing adjustments are allowed when needed for responsive composition; avoid unapproved decorative structures.
- Use the approved DOM/CSS/images approach. Keep text, controls, hotspots, and information panels in semantic HTML; use images for scenery and original artwork.
- Build only the simple idle, walk, jump, and interaction states required by the plan. Use original spritesheets and CSS animation; do not introduce physics, complex mechanics, or an engine.

## Asset handling and provenance

- Organize assets by purpose and use descriptive, stable filenames. Keep original source files when optimizing or exporting variants.
- Preserve crisp pixel edges and appropriate intrinsic dimensions. Check how each asset crops or scales in desktop, tablet, and mobile scene framing.
- Create assets specifically for this project. Do not copy or trace recognizable characters, maps, sprites, logos, objects, UI, music, sound effects, or other protected material from games or franchises.
- Review generated images for recognizable similarities before use; generation alone does not establish originality or legal clearance. For third-party material, verify and record source and license before inclusion.
- Do not claim an asset is legally safe without appropriate verification. Escalate meaningful uncertainty rather than silently changing the approved identity.
